Frequently Asked Questions

Answers regarding our mobile car upholstery deep cleaning.

What do you need on-site to clean my vehicle?

We only require access to an ordinary 220V household electrical plug point within 30 metres of your vehicle and an outdoor water tap. Our mobile van carries all extension cords, hoses, pressure extraction machinery, and specialized detailing tools.

How long do car seats take to dry?

Typically 2 to 4 hours. Our high-suction vacuum nozzles extract over 85% of applied moisture. Keeping windows lowered slightly allows warm Cape Town air to circulate and finish drying rapidly.

Can you clean leather car seats as well?

While our primary machine extraction is engineered for fabric and velour car upholstery, we can safely wipe down and condition genuine and synthetic leather surfaces using specialized pH-neutral leather conditioners upon request.

How do I get a price quote for my vehicle?

Tell us your vehicle make and model (e.g., hatchback, sedan, SUV, double cab) or send a quick photo on WhatsApp at 067 735 2364. We will send an instant fixed quote with no hidden extras.
