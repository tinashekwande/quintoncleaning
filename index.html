Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our cleaning methods, drying times, and booking process.

How long do couches and carpets take to dry after deep cleaning?

With our dual-stage industrial vacuum extraction equipment, fabric couches and carpets typically dry within 3 to 6 hours. Adequate room ventilation, natural air flow, or running a fan accelerates the drying process.

What cleaning services does Quinton Chinoz Projects provide?

We specialise in on-site deep cleaning for couches and upholstery, fitted carpets and area rugs, mattress sanitisation, tile and grout deep scrubbing, and vehicle interior upholstery.

Are your cleaning products safe for pets and young children?

Yes. We exclusively use non-toxic, eco-friendly, biodegradable cleaning solutions that leave zero harsh or hazardous chemical residues, ensuring safety for your family and pets.

Which areas in Cape Town do you cover?

Based in Kraaifontein, our mobile vans service all of the Northern Suburbs (Kraaifontein, Brackenfell, Durbanville, Bellville, Kuils River, Parow, Goodwood), Southern Suburbs, West Coast, and Greater Cape Town. See our full Areas Served guide.

How do I request an upfront quote?

Simply send a quick photo of your items or room on WhatsApp to 067 735 2364 or fill out our online quote form below. We respond promptly with fixed, transparent pricing.
