Why Mattresses Need Deep Extraction

Frequently Asked Questions

What to know before scheduling your mattress deep clean.

How long does a mattress take to dry?

Most mattresses dry within 3 to 6 hours. If we clean your mattress in the morning, opening a window or running a fan ensures it is completely dry, deodorised, and ready for clean bedding by bedtime.

Can you clean both sides of the mattress?

Yes. If your mattress is double-sided, we can clean both the top and bottom surfaces, as well as the perimeter side bolsters. We prop the mattress upright during post-clean inspection to ensure airflow to both faces.

Will deep cleaning remove yellow sweat and urine marks?

Yes. We apply enzymatic stain lifters that target biological stains and break down the dried proteins and uric acid crystals. While stains that are years old and have chemically discoloured delicate cotton may not always vanish 100%, they are heavily faded and 100% sanitised hygienically.
