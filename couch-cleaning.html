The Method

Our 5-Step Deep Cleaning Process

We follow a methodical, fabric-safe cleaning sequence engineered for maximum hygiene and fast turnaround.

1

Fabric Inspection

We test fabric fibre type, colour fastness, and identify stubborn spots, traffic areas, and spillages to choose the optimal pH balance.

2

Dry Soil Evacuation

High-suction vacuuming dislodges dry sand, grit, crumbs, and hair from seams, button tufts, and beneath seat cushions.

3

Targeted Pre-Treatment

Eco-friendly emulsifiers are sprayed and gently agitated with soft upholstery brushes to break down stubborn grease, body oils, and stains.

4

Deep Injection-Extraction

Clean sanitising solution is injected deep into the weave under pressure and immediately extracted with powerful dual-stage vacuum suction.

5

Speed Drying & Groom

Fabric fibres are set in place, excess moisture is extracted to ensure rapid 3–6 hour drying, and the item is inspected for complete satisfaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers regarding our couch and upholstery deep cleaning service in Cape Town.

How long does a fabric couch take to dry after deep cleaning?

With our dual-stage industrial vacuum moisture extraction equipment, most fabric couches dry completely within 3 to 6 hours. Factors like room ventilation, weather, and fabric density play a role. Leaving windows slightly open or turning on a ceiling/standing fan noticeably speeds up drying.

Can you remove tough stains like coffee, red wine, and pet urine?

Yes. We use targeted enzymatic and bio-safe stain lifters specifically formulated for food, grease, wine, juice, and pet accidents. While stains that have chemically bleached or permanently bonded to fibers cannot always be 100% reversed, our multi-stage extraction successfully removes over 95% of common household stains and neutralises sub-surface odours.

Are your cleaning detergents safe for kids and pets?

Yes. Quinton Chinoz Projects exclusively uses eco-friendly, biodegradable, and non-caustic solutions. They contain no harsh bleaching agents or toxic residues, ensuring that once dry, your couch is completely safe and gentle for babies, toddlers, and pets.

Do you clean on-site in my home or take the furniture away?

All cleaning is performed conveniently on-site at your home or office. Our fully-equipped mobile team brings all industrial extraction machinery, hoses, and eco-friendly products directly to your doorstep anywhere in Cape Town.
